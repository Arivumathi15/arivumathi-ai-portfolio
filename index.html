Multi-Agent AI Sales Pipeline — Multi-Tenant Migration

Led the migration of a production multi-agent sales-automation pipeline from a single-tenant prototype to a multi-tenant platform. Six cooperating agents (Universal, Dispatch, Action, Reasoning, Memory, Event Logger) coordinate over NATS JetStream; I redesigned the messaging topology to carry a full tenant identity through every hop, then root-caused a cross-agent bug that was silently dropping tenant identifiers mid-chain — restoring end-to-end propagation without touching the core decision logic. Validated the rebuilt platform end-to-end with a Postman test suite against the live message bus.
